Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your device, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select CAFC Digest, and cancel there.

Where does the case data come from?
CAFC Digest summarizes opinions published by the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit. Summaries are for informational purposes only and are not legal advice — always verify against the official opinion.
