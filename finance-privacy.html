# Privacy Policy — Finance Workbench Sync (iPhone) _Last updated: October 21, 2026_ Finance Workbench Sync ("the app") is the iPhone companion of Finance Workbench, a personal finance app. The app is published by **[organization name]**, which this policy calls "we". ## What the app accesses With your permission, granted in Apple's FinanceKit authorization sheet, the app reads the following from Apple Wallet: - the accounts, balances and transactions of your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Apple Savings accounts; - transaction descriptions, merchant names and categories, amounts, currencies, dates and status; - account names, the issuing institution, and credit limit and payment-due information. You choose which accounts to share. You can withdraw access at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone. ## What the app does with it - The app reads this data only when you tap **Export**. - It writes the data to a file on your iPhone. - The file leaves your iPhone only when you choose to save or share it, for example to your own iCloud Drive or by AirDrop to your own Mac. There it can be read by the Finance Workbench app. - Before writing the file, the app masks anything that looks like a card or account number to its last four digits. ## What we do not do - We do not collect, receive or store your data. The app has no servers, no accounts, no network code, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. - We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising, profiling or credit decisions. - The app does not run in the background or on a schedule. ## Storage and deletion Export files and the app's sync bookmarks are stored on your iPhone with iOS data protection. They are unreadable while the device is locked. To delete them: - Delete the files under **Files › On My iPhone › Finance Workbench Sync**, or remove the app. - Files you saved or shared elsewhere are under your control at their destination. ## Children The app is not directed to children. ## Changes and contact We will post any change to this policy here, with a new date.
