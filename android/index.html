Frequently asked questions

Where does the case data come from?
Summaries describe publicly available opinions from the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit. AI-generated summaries can contain errors. Always verify against the official opinion; summaries are informational and are not legal advice.

How do notifications work?
The app periodically checks for new cases and creates notifications on your device. Allow notifications in Android app settings. Background checks may be delayed by Android battery or network restrictions; use Refresh to check manually.

How do I share an opinion PDF?
Open a case and select PDF, Email, or Share. An internet connection is needed to download an uncached PDF. Select a compatible PDF viewer or sharing app. Your chosen app handles delivery.

Is there a subscription?
The current Android version has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

How do I delete local app data?
Use Android Settings → Apps → CAFC Digest → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes cached summaries, preferences, notification state, and app-managed attachments. Files copied or messages sent through other apps must be removed separately.
