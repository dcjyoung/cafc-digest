Privacy Policy — CAFC Digest for Android

Effective and last updated October 10, 2026

CAFC Digest for Android provides AI-generated summaries of publicly available Federal Circuit opinions. James Young operates the app. This policy applies to the Android version.

No account required

You can use the app without an account. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, or advertising identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs and does not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

On-device data

Search is performed on your device. Search text and reading state are held locally; case-summary caches, disclaimer acceptance, refresh timestamps, and previously seen case identifiers are stored in private app storage. Opinion PDFs and generated sharing attachments are stored in the app's cache. These may remain until the cache is cleared, Android removes cached files, or you uninstall the app. Android backup of app data is disabled. The app does not upload search terms or reading history. This version has no translation feature.

Network requests and service providers

The app retrieves case summaries from JSONBin.io over HTTPS, including during background refresh. Opinion PDFs are downloaded from the linked opinion host, commonly the court's website. These providers receive ordinary request information, including your IP address and requested resource, and may retain server logs under their own policies. The app does not send them a personal account, advertising identifier, or notification token.

Support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, subject to GitHub's privacy statement. Opening source, podcast, LinkedIn, or other external links connects to those services through your selected browser or app. Their privacy policies govern their handling of information.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and are generated locally after periodic feed checks. This Android version does not use remote push notifications, register a push token, or upload a device token. Android controls permission and delivery, and may delay background checks. You can disable notifications in Android app settings.

Email and sharing

When you choose Email or Share, the app prepares the selected case summary and links and, when available, an opinion PDF. It passes this content to the app you select and grants temporary read access to the attachment. CAFC Digest does not send email itself or collect your selected recipients. Your chosen app controls sending, storage, recipients, and further sharing under its own policies.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Email is handled by the email service provider. We retain correspondence as needed to handle the request and maintain support records; you may request deletion by contacting storage.bawl6r@icloud.com, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Payments

An optional one-time supporter purchase uses Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; the app and developer do not receive your card details. The app receives a signed purchase record, product identifier, purchase token and payment status, verifies the record locally, and sends an acknowledgement to Google Play. It stores supporter status on your device and checks ownership with Google Play to restore purchases and recognize refunds. Purchase records are not sent to a developer-operated server. All case features remain available without purchasing. Google Play has its own terms and privacy practices.

Deletion and choices

Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove app-managed local data. You can clear cached attachments using Android's Clear cache option. Copies saved or shared through other apps are controlled by those apps. Because the app has no user account or server-side notification token, there is no app account or push-token record to delete. Contact us for requests concerning support correspondence.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for legal professionals and researchers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support correspondence, please contact us.

Changes

We will update this page and its last-updated date when the Android app's data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email storage.bawl6r@icloud.com.
